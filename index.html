What Is BPM?

BPM means Beats Per Minute. It tells you how many beats happen in one minute and is the standard way musicians describe tempo.

GenreBPM Range
Slow Ballads60–80 BPM
Hip-Hop70–100 BPM
Pop Music100–130 BPM
House Music120–130 BPM
EDM128–150 BPM
Drum & Bass160–180 BPM
