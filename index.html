What Is BPM?

BPM stands for Beats Per Minute. It tells you how many beats occur in one minute and is the standard way musicians describe tempo.

A song at 60 BPM has one beat every second, while a song at 120 BPM has two beats every second.

Genre Typical BPM
Slow Ballads 60–80 BPM
Hip-Hop 70–100 BPM
Pop Music 100–130 BPM
House Music 120–130 BPM
EDM 128–150 BPM
Drum & Bass 160–180 BPM
